Albums to Check Out

Five albums from five different artists

Home Artists Albums

Pink Floyd - Wish You Were Here (1975)

This album has a slow, atmospheric sound and deals with absence and distance. The opening parts of “Shine On You Crazy Diamond” are a good example of how the band builds a mood over time.

Kanye West - The College Dropout (2004)

This album uses soul samples and gospel influences. It combines funny stories with more serious topics like ambition, money, and expectations about success.

MF DOOM - MM..FOOD (2004)

This album uses food as a theme for jokes and wordplay. The production has lots of samples, and the lyrics are worth listening to closely because there are so many details.

Radiohead - Kid A (2000)

Radiohead moved away from their earlier guitar sound on this album and used more electronic sounds. It has a strange atmosphere that makes it feel different from a typical rock album.

Tyler, The Creator - IGOR (2019)

This album mixes hip-hop with soul and synth-pop. The songs connect together and follow a narrator dealing with complicated feelings about a relationship.

My Listening Tip

Try listening to an album from beginning to end without shuffling. You might notice connections between songs that you miss when listening to a playlist.
