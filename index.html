Welcome!

Music is a big part of how people express themselves. I like artists who have their own sound and make albums that feel different from one another. This website introduces five artists I enjoy listening to: Pink Floyd, Kanye West, MF DOOM, Radiohead, and Tyler, The Creator.

Use the links at the top to learn about the artists and check out a few albums to listen to.

The Artists

Pink Floyd

Known for progressive rock, long songs, and atmospheric sounds.

Kanye West

Known for creative production and changing his sound between albums.

MF DOOM

An underground rapper known for wordplay and his metal mask.

Radiohead

A band that mixes alternative rock with electronic and experimental music.

Tyler, The Creator

A rapper and producer who combines hip-hop, soul, and colorful album concepts.

Why I Like Albums

I like listening to an album all the way through instead of only listening to individual songs. The order of the tracks, the production, and the mood can make an album feel like one complete piece of art.
